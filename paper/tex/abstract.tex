A small comparison in a diffusion personalisation project was recorded as a
mechanism being ruled out: composing two separately trained attention adapters
raised a dispersion statistic relative to training the same parameters jointly,
and the project logged the comparison under a label marking its evaluation as
unpaired. We re-read the artifacts that comparison was computed from and find
that the pairing was never missing from the experiment. All three arms were
generated over the same \NPairs{} background prompts, from adapters trained at
the same budget, learning rate, rank, resolution and seed, and the two composed
adapters train exactly the \JointParams{} parameters that the joint arm trains
as one. What discarded the pairing was the endpoint: a dispersion across
prompts is one number per arm, so the comparison that was registered has no
pairs left to test, whatever the runs did.

Three things follow, all computable from the recorded bytes without generating
an image. First, taken on its own terms as a comparison of two dispersions
estimated from \NPairs{} generations each, the recorded result supports a ratio
anywhere from \DispLower{} to \DispUpper{} at 95\% confidence; it contains no
difference, a halving, and a near fivefold increase alike. Second, the pairing
can be recovered at the level the dispersion was computed from, and when it is,
the composed arm is lower on \IdentityDown{} of \NPairs{} pairs in identity
fidelity and on \PromptDown{} of \NPairs{} in prompt fidelity, at sign-test
probabilities of \IdentitySignP{} and \PromptSignP{}. Third, the reason the
second of those does not reach the conventional threshold is not the data: with
\NPairs{} pairs the smallest two-sided probability an exact sign test can
return is \FloorAtFour{}, so the test could not have produced a significant
result however the pairs had fallen. The only analysis of these runs that
crosses \Alpha{} is a paired $t$ test on an endpoint that was not the
registered one, and it does so by assuming a distributional shape that four
points cannot exhibit.

We therefore state the recorded finding at the strength its evidence carries
--- no separation observed in one small comparison whose registered endpoint
admits no paired test --- and pre-register the experiment that would settle it:
\DesignPerConcept{} pairs per concept across \DesignConcepts{} concepts,
defined at generation time by concept, prompt and seed, sized so that
\DesignTolerance{} pairs may fall the wrong way and the test can still reach
\Alpha{}. The protocol is the deliverable. It is not run here, the reference
table it would eventually be compared against remains unproduced, and the
recorded gate for the unrun lane still stands at
\AuthorisationState{}.
